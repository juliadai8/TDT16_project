PHOTO RECONSTRUCTION PROTOTYPE

Open Assets/Archived/DinosaurDemo/DinosaurPhotoDemo.unity and press Play.
Right-drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. The panel is visible in Play mode.

The point shader is included in Archived/DinosaurDemo/Resources.
See the repository README.md and this demo's Reconstruction/README.md for setup,
source attribution, reconstruction method, calibration, and limitations.

Archived reconstruction scripts and dataset notes are in the Reconstruction/
subfolder next to this file. Follow this demo's Reconstruction/README.md
for commands and dependencies. Raw downloads and outputs remain outside Assets.
